01 / Philosophy in practice

Real engagement is more than attention.

I want pupils to ask questions, take intellectual risks, make things, move, laugh and see themselves as capable learners.

My approach combines high expectations with meaningful experiences. I am interested in purposeful innovation: lessons and projects that deepen understanding, grow confidence and remain memorable long after they finish.

These examples span creative literacy, collaborative projects, technology, outdoor education and international service learning.

04 / International service learning · Tanzania

Service, partnership
and perspective.

A service-learning expedition with Year 12 pupils from the International School of Lausanne, bringing together education, community engagement and practical experience.

The expedition involved school-based activities, a community construction project, microfinance learning, and a range of shared experiences. My involvement included pastoral support and safeguarding alongside logistical planning.

Learning together

Classroom workshops, creative activities and exchanges with pupils in a different educational setting.

Practical contribution

Community project work offered opportunities for cooperation, responsibility and reflection on the nature of meaningful service.

Shared experiences

Sport, everyday encounters and listening to local perspectives helped place the work in a wider human context.
