01 · About

A teacher first.
A leader through service.

I have built my career around strong relationships, high expectations and a belief that children learn best when academic challenge is balanced with curiosity, movement, wellbeing and meaningful experience.

My work has ranged from Year 4 PYP teaching and Primary-to-Middle School transition to mathematics intervention, curriculum development, staff mentoring, residential learning and sport. I am most effective in schools where adults are reflective, collaborative and willing to improve what they do for pupils.

02 · Leadership

“The strongest captains are not the loudest. They are part of the team, set the standard and help others do their best work.”

My approach to educational leadership has been shaped as much by sport as by schools.

01

Visible

Present with pupils and colleagues, connected to the everyday life of the school.

02

Supportive

Build trust, listen well and give people the confidence to grow.

03

Reflective

Try, evaluate and refine rather than protecting systems simply because they are familiar.

04

Child-centred

Keep decisions anchored in learning, wellbeing and what pupils genuinely need next.
