Teaching is a big part of who I am. I love the unexpected questions, the moments when something finally clicks and seeing pupils discover what they're capable of.

For nearly twenty years, I've brought that enthusiasm to classrooms, sports fields, school trips and the wider life of international schools. This portfolio is a glimpse of the experiences and people who have shaped me as an educator.

01 · About

A teacher first.
Involved in the whole school.

I've always enjoyed being involved beyond my own classroom. My work has taken me from Year 4 and Grade 5 teaching to mathematics intervention, curriculum development, school trips and pastoral mentoring.

I set high expectations, but I also want pupils to feel comfortable asking for help or having another go. Some of the most rewarding conversations I've had with students have happened in corridors, on trips or in the fitness centre.
